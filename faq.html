Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before getting started.

What is SME Growth Advisor?
SME Growth Advisor is a platform that helps Malaysian small and medium businesses grow and digitalise. We combine a free AI-powered business assessment with a curated marketplace of digital solutions and access to vetted human advisors.
Is the business assessment really free?
Yes. The assessment is 100% free with no credit card required, and takes about 5–7 minutes. You'll get an instant preview of your Growth Score, and creating a free account unlocks your full personalised report.
How does the AI Growth Advisor work?
The AI Growth Advisor analyses your assessment answers across six key areas — strategy, sales & marketing, operations, finance, people, and digitalisation — and surfaces the highest-impact opportunities and solutions for your specific business, rather than generic advice.
Can solution providers list their products on the platform?
Yes. Software vendors and service providers can apply for a Provider account, submit listings for review, and get matched with SMEs whose assessment results show a genuine fit for their solution.
How are advisors vetted?
Every advisor goes through a background and experience review by our Advisor Network team before being approved. We look for real, relevant SME consulting experience — not just credentials.
Is my business data secure?
Your assessment answers and company information are kept private and are only used to generate your personalised insights and recommendations. We never sell your data to third parties.
Which industries and regions do you support?
We support SMEs across all major industries — manufacturing, retail, F&B, professional services, construction, and more — and across all states in Malaysia, with an advisor and solution network that continues to grow.
How do I get started?
Just click "Assess My Business" to take the free assessment. You'll get instant results, and can create a free account afterwards to unlock your full report, solution matches, and advisor recommendations.
