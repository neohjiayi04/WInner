Our Story

SME Growth Advisor started in 2022 when a small group of operators and technologists kept running into the same problem: Malaysian SMEs had no shortage of digital tools to choose from, but no easy way to know which ones actually fit their business — or who to trust for advice.

So we built a platform that starts with a simple, free assessment, layers on AI-powered insights, and connects business owners with vetted advisors and solution providers who can actually help. Today we work with hundreds of SMEs and advisors across Malaysia, and we're just getting started.
